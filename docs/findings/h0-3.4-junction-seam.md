## What remained (measured on `9b72697d5c52`, Turnout L02 · CH 2060 m, RL 80 m)

Walking to the turnout (Go To → "Turnout L02 · CH 2060 m") and looking into
the opening showed, at the RAMP_ACCESS junction:

1. a thin **black triangular sliver on the floor** along the seam between the
   ramp floor and the clipped access floor (a hole — the void below was
   visible), and
2. **wall / floor fragments standing inside the opening** between the ramp
   wall edge and the access tube (lit textured triangles, not a lighting
   artefact).

### Root cause (geometry dump of `RAMP_JUNCTION:L02`, PR-2)

The Phase 20D.1.2 child floor clip keeps the outside-parent remainder of
every straddling child FLOOR quad. Its boundary chord lies on the parent's
**tolerance surface** — `JUNCTION_SURFACE_TOLERANCE_FRACTION × width` =
0.10 m outside the ramp WALL — at the CHILD floor elevation, while the ramp
floor ends at the wall base. Because the rule-188 branch profile matches the
ramp floor along the traffic centerline only, the retained child floor beyond
the wall sits above the ramp floor by up to `g·(λ − w/2)·tan φ`. Measured on
L02 the open strip was 0.10–0.29 m wide in plan and −0.09…+0.16 m in rise
along ≈ 10 m — the sliver. Separately, child WALL / ROOF quads straddling the
parent boundary were kept whole (only child FLOOR quads were clipped), so
their inside-parent halves stood in the opening — the fragments.

## Fix (PR-2 C4, `design/junctions.py`)

1. **Sills.** Every clipped child floor chord that is a floor meeting of the
   documented kind — within `SILL_MAX_PLAN_DISTANCE_WIDTHS × width` (0.1 ·
   width = 0.5 m) in plan of the parent FLOOR-EDGE polyline and with a rise
   below the parent wall's middle sample row (`PARENT_QUAD_SAMPLE_FRACTIONS`
   middle × wall height = 1.25 m for the 2.5 m wall) — is bridged by ONE sill
   quad to its feet on the parent floor edge (a real step surface between two
   real floors, oriented away from the parent void, degenerate loops merged).
   A chord that is not such a meeting gets no sill and is COUNTED
   (`FloorClip.chords_without_sill`, report `childChordsWithoutSill`), never
   invented. `FloorClip.sill_chords` records which chord each sill bridges.
2. **Child wall clipping.** Straddling child WALL / ROOF quads are clipped
   by the same `_clip_floor_quad(floor=False)` remainder rule as the floor
   quads (`JunctionCut.wall_clips`); a quad the clipper cannot triangulate is
   counted (`unclipped_wall_quads`) instead of kept whole.
3. Parent side unchanged (the ramp wall opens, the ramp floor stays:
   `parentWallTriangles > 0`, `parentFloorTriangles == 0`). The logical
   meshes, their QA, every centerline, every threshold, gate and golden are
   unchanged; `renderOmittedTriangles` / `renderReplacementTriangles` now
   include the clipped walls and the sills, and the additive report fields
   `renderClippedWallQuads`, `renderSillTriangles`, `childClippedWallQuads`,
   `childUnclippedWallQuads`, `childSillTriangles`, `childChordsWithoutSill`
   make the counts inspectable.
